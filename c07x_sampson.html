Our Featured Guest

February — Scott Sampson

How can a dinosaur that lived millions of years ago still capture our imagination today? Join Dr. Scott Sampson at the Saroyan to discover the fascinating history of Utahceratops and the incredible diversity of prehistoric life.

Scott Sampson is a Canadian-born paleontologist who earned his doctorate in zoology from the University of Toronto. His research examined horned dinosaurs from the Late Cretaceous period, with particular attention to their horns, frills, growth, and anatomy.

After completing his studies, Sampson worked at the American Museum of Natural History in New York City. He later taught anatomy at the New York College of Osteopathic Medicine before joining the University of Utah as a professor and vertebrate paleontology curator.

His work explores dinosaur evolution, physical characteristics, and relationships among species. His paleontological fieldwork has included locations in Zimbabwe, South Africa, Madagascar, Canada, and the United States.

Sampson has also appeared as the host of the Discovery Channel's Dinosaur Planet. His book, Dinosaur Odyssey: Fossil Threads in the Web of Life, explores dinosaurs and the ecosystems in which they lived.
